Stare

Watch the satellites

In italiano

Point your phone at the sky and Stare draws the satellites passing over you, on the picture of the sky in front of you, where they actually are.

Before the first launch

Stare downloads a 95 MB model the first time it opens, which is what lets it tell sky from buildings. Give it a minute on Wi-Fi before pointing it anywhere. It is downloaded once and kept.

It is not finding anything

  • You are indoors. Anything behind a wall, a roof, a building or a tree is deliberately left out rather than drawn on top of it. Under a ceiling that is nearly everything. Go outside.
  • The compass has not settled. Move the phone in a figure of eight, away from anything magnetic — a car dashboard and a magnetic case are the usual culprits.
  • It is daylight. The satellites are still drawn where they are, but almost none of them can be seen by eye until an hour or two after sunset.

It says a permission is missing

Stare needs the camera and your location, and it says so before it asks. iOS raises each prompt once per install, so if one was refused the only way back is Settings › Stare on the phone. The app's own error screen links there directly.

Reporting something

The whole app is open source. Bugs, questions and suggestions all go to the issue tracker. If you are reporting something that went wrong, the Console page in Settings holds the figures worth photographing — the build number especially.
