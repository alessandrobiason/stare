Privacy Policy

Stare · last updated 20 September 2026

In italiano

Stare has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to its developer, and no data about you is collected.

What the app reads from your phone

  • Your location. Read while the app is open, to work out which satellites are above you and where on the sky to draw them. It is used on the device and never transmitted. It is not written to storage: close the app and it is gone.
  • The camera. The live preview is the picture the markers are drawn over. Single frames are analysed on the device, by a model that runs on the device, to tell sky from buildings and trees so that anything behind them can be left out. A frame is written to the app's private cache for as long as that analysis takes and deleted immediately afterwards. Frames are never uploaded and never added to your photo library.
  • Motion and compass sensors. Read to know which way the phone is pointing. They stay on the device.
  • Notifications. Pass alerts are local notifications — a time and a message handed to iOS by the app itself. No push server is involved and no device token is ever registered or sent anywhere.

What the app downloads

Stare makes requests to the four services below. None of them is sent your location, your camera frames, or any identifier the app has made up. As with any request over the internet, each of them does receive your device's IP address and can see that a request was made.

  • celestrak.org — the public catalogue of tracked objects and their orbits, refreshed from time to time. The request asks for the catalogue and says nothing about you.
  • huggingface.co — the sky-detection model, downloaded once on first launch and then kept on the device.
  • commons.wikimedia.org and upload.wikimedia.org — photographs of a few well-known spacecraft, fetched when you tap one that has one.
  • u.expo.dev — asks whether a newer version of the app's own code has been published, so that fixes can arrive without a new download from the App Store. The request carries the app's version and the platform it is running on.

What is kept on your phone

The downloaded catalogue and the time it was fetched, the sky-detection model, the language you picked if you picked one, and whether you have been shown the tour. All of it lives in the app's own storage and all of it goes when the app is deleted.

Children

Stare is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If what the app does with any of this changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.

Contact

Stare is built in the open. Questions and corrections are welcome at the project's issue tracker, and the source for everything described here is in the same repository.
